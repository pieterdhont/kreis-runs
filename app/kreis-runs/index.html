The runner

His arms, legs and hakama are single continuous meshes that bend smoothly at the elbows and knees. A running gait based on real biomechanics drives the hips, knees, ankles and toes, with a soft give as each foot lands, a push-off at the back and a short flight between strides. His arms swing against his legs with trailing elbows, and his hips and shoulders twist against each other.

His look

A Japanese-meets-Bauhaus hero, cel-shaded with ink outlines. He has a flame-coloured crest of hair and a wrap top with a family crest. A black obi with streaming tails and red-lined sleeves with ribbons complete the outfit, along with pleated hakama and a cape split into two tails. He blinks, and his eyes narrow, brows set and mouth opens as he runs faster.

The camera

Every angle can circle a full 360° around Kreis: turn on 360° circle, or drag the scene to orbit and tilt by hand. Zoom with the scroll wheel, a pinch, the slider or + −. Director cuts between seven shots, each with its own zoom move. Reset view returns to the starting position.

Shortcuts
