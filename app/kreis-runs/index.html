The runner

Jointed hips, knees, ankles, shoulders and elbows driven by a running cycle. A crest of faceted, flame-like locks sweeps back from his brow, flattening and fluttering as he speeds up, while his red cloak streams behind.

The camera

Zoom works on every angle, from an eye-level close-up of his face out to a wide aerial view of the land. Use the scroll wheel, a two-finger pinch, the slider or + −. Double-click the scene to reset. Director cuts between seven shots on its own, each with its own zoom move.

Cinema tools

Depth of field keeps Kreis in focus and blurs the world around him. Letterbox frames a 2.39:1 widescreen view. Handheld adds a gentle sway, Slow push breathes the zoom in and out, Dutch tilts the horizon, and Dolly zoom stretches the world behind him while he holds his size.

Shortcuts

Space pause, 1–6 cameras, D day or dusk, Z dolly zoom, 0 reset zoom.
